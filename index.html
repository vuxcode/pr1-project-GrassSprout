Ultimate Tic Tac Toe

Red vs. Blue
Blue starts!

RULES:
Click on any button to start!
The button you press corresponds with a board, try to click in that corresponding board!
Ex: Middle button is always the middle board
If you win a board you get to choose any board you want!
Try to get 3 boards in a row to win!
To play again you need to reset the page!

Good Luck!
